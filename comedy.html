STILL ALIVE

By: Samay Raina

Experience Samay Raina live with Still Alive, a stand-up comedy show built around sharp observations, spontaneous humour, storytelling, and Samay's unmistakable style. Known for his work in Indian comedy and his love for chess, Samay brings a highly personal and largely unscripted performance to the stage. Still Alive reflects his journey through an eventful period and his return to live comedy, turning real experiences into an energetic evening of laughter. Catch Samay Raina live on 12 October 2026.
